Lockup rule
Art Beyond Age stands as an independent brand. The wordmark should always appear unencumbered — never alongside another logo at equal or competing scale. The sub-mark ("Creative Aging & Wellness") may be omitted in compact applications.
05 — Voice & Tone
Quiet authority. Warm invitation.
We are
  • Gentle — never patronising
  • Poetic — not clinical or technical
  • Inviting — never pressuring
  • Curious — always hopeful
  • Dignified — adults, not patients
We are not
  • Therapeutic or medical in tone
  • Infantilising ("seniors", "elderly")
  • Urgent or fear-based ("slow decline")
  • Trendy or youthful for its own sake
  • Loud, promotional, or salesy

07 — Brand Conduct
Do this. Not that.
Do
  • Use Cormorant for all headline moments
  • Let white space breathe — never fill every gap
  • Refer to participants as "adults" or by name
  • Use Clay as a neutral accent in UI and tags
  • Keep imagery soft, natural, hands-focused
  • Use Dusk and Sage as the primary accent pair
Don't
  • Use "senior citizens" or "the elderly" in copy
  • Use Clay as a dominant hero colour — it is neutral
  • Use sans-serif for display text — it loses warmth
  • Use stock images of passive, grey-haired figures
  • Apply clinical language ("therapy", "treatment")
  • Crowd the wordmark — always allow clear space
